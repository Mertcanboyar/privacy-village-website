The village is raising its youngest defenders

Today's students will spend their whole lives among golems — AI that talks, watches, remembers, and decides. Nobody taught our generation how to live with that. We can do better for theirs.

Privacy Village Teens brings the village's quest-based world to students aged 13–18: privacy, AI literacy, and cybersecurity, taught the way young people actually want to learn — by playing. The same medieval world, the same learning-by-doing, tuned for the classroom.

Built the only way a privacy lawyer would build it

Designed for classrooms. Not for data.

Privacy Village was founded by a privacy lawyer and privacy engineer — so the teen village is built the way we teach others to build:

Teachers hold the accounts

Students never register. Classes join a session with a class code — no student sign-ups, no student emails, no student profiles.

We don't track students

No behavioral tracking, no advertising, no profiles built on minors. We collect what a lesson needs and nothing more.

No open chat

Students play within their teacher's room. The teen village and the adult village are entirely separate worlds — never a shared space.

Radical data minimization, published openly

Every design decision about what we chose not to collect is documented in public. Hold us to it.

“The safest data is the data never collected.” That isn't just our compliance posture — it's the first lesson of the curriculum.
